# developing Human Connectome Project (dHCP) Neonatal

The dHCP is the highest-quality open neonatal brain MRI dataset, designed to map structural and functional connectivity development across 20-45 weeks post-menstrual age. The second public release contains 887 datasets from 783 neonates scanned on a Philips Achieva 3T using a custom 32-channel neonatal head coil, with motion-tolerant acquisition pipelines for T1w, T2w, dMRI (300 directions across 4 shells), and resting-state fMRI. Approximate total volume is 500 GB. The dHCP processing pipeline is included alongside raw and minimally-preprocessed images.

## Access and tier

Registration with institutional email at https://www.developingconnectome.org/. Sign data sharing agreement.

## Format and download

Native: NIfTI / BIDS. Recommended download path: Academic Torrents (preferred for bandwidth) or direct download via dHCP portal.

## Intended use for this corpus

Best-in-class fetal/neonatal brain MRI for tissue segmentation, cortical surface modeling, gestational-age prediction, neurodevelopmental biomarker discovery, and motion-robust reconstruction research. Pairs with FeTA for in-utero coverage.
