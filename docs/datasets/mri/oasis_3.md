# OASIS-3 (Longitudinal Aging/Alzheimer's)

OASIS-3 is the third release of the Open Access Series of Imaging Studies, a retrospective compilation from Washington University's Knight ADRC spanning 30 years. It includes 1378 participants (755 cognitively normal, 622 across various stages of cognitive decline) ages 42-95, with 2842 MRI sessions covering T1w MPRAGE, T2w, FLAIR, ASL, SWI, time-of-flight angiography, resting-state BOLD, and DTI. Many sessions include FreeSurfer volumetric segmentation derivatives. The dataset additionally provides 2157 PET scans across PIB amyloid, AV45 amyloid, and FDG, plus 6534 longitudinal clinical assessments and 3410 neuropsychological assessments.

## Access and tier

Registration (free). Sign OASIS Data Use Agreement and create NITRC account; data hosted on Central XNAT (https://central.xnat.org/).

## Format and download

Native: NIfTI + DICOM. Recommended: NIfTI plus FreeSurfer derivatives (already provided). Per-session zips via XNAT or scriptable XNAT REST API.

## Intended use for this corpus

Longitudinal AD progression modeling, multimodal MRI-PET fusion, FreeSurfer-supervised cortical analysis, and as a registration-tier bridge between IXI (healthy) and ADNI (application-only). OASIS-4 (663 dementia-evaluated subjects) is a complementary release.

## License

OASIS Data Use Agreement; free for academic research. Avid Radiopharmaceuticals review required for publications using AV45 PET.
