# Duke Breast Cancer MRI (TCIA)

The Duke-Breast-Cancer-MRI collection contains pre-operative dynamic contrast-enhanced (DCE) MRI from 922 biopsy-confirmed invasive breast cancer patients treated at Duke Hospital between 2000 and 2014. Imaging includes axial bilateral acquisitions on 1.5T or 3T scanners with non-fat-saturated T1w, fat-saturated gradient-echo T1 pre-contrast, and three-to-four post-contrast T1 sequences. The collection totals 773,253 DICOM images across 922 patients (368.89 GB) and is accompanied by demographic, clinical, pathology (ER/PR/HER2), treatment, outcomes, and a genomics subset.

## Access and tier

Open. Hosted on The Cancer Imaging Archive (TCIA). Click-through agreement; no application.

## Format and download

Native: DICOM. Recommended: DICOM-to-NIfTI per series. Use TCIA NBIA Data Retriever or `tcia-cli` for bulk retrieval. IBM Aspera Connect required for large packages.

## Intended use for this corpus

Breast tumor segmentation, DCE-MRI kinetic feature learning, response prediction, and radiogenomic models linking imaging phenotypes to molecular subtype. Largest open DCE-MRI breast cohort.
