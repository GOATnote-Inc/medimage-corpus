# CHAOS Combined Healthy Abdominal Organ (MRI subset)

CHAOS is an established abdominal organ segmentation benchmark from ISBI 2019 (companion CT cases at 40 patients). The MRI portion includes 40 patients (23 male / 17 female, ages 18-76, mean 54.6) scanned with two pulse sequences: T1-DUAL (in-phase and opposed-phase, 40 datasets each) and T2-SPIR fat-saturated (40 datasets), totaling 120 DICOM datasets. Annotations cover four abdominal organs: liver, spleen, right kidney, left kidney.

## Access and tier

Registration via the Grand Challenge platform; signed letter of intent. Zenodo mirror at DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.3362845.

## Format and download

Native: DICOM. Recommended: DICOM-to-NIfTI per series with paired T1-in/T1-out/T2-SPIR channels.

## Intended use for this corpus

Multi-organ abdominal segmentation, cross-modality CT-MR transfer (the CHAOS CT-MRI pairing is the established benchmark for this task), in-phase/opposed-phase Dixon-like learning.
