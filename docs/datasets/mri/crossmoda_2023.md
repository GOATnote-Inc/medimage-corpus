# crossMoDA 2023 (Vestibular Schwannoma Cross-Modality)

crossMoDA is the premier MICCAI unsupervised domain-adaptation challenge for medical imaging, focused on segmenting vestibular schwannomas (VS) and cochlea using contrast-free high-resolution T2 (hrT2) imaging by transferring knowledge from contrast-enhanced T1 (ceT1) data. The 2023 edition incorporates multi-institutional, heterogeneous routine surveillance data and adds a 3-class sub-segmentation (intra-meatal tumor, extra-meatal tumor, cochlea). Training: 227 ceT1 + 295 unpaired hrT2 subjects; validation: 96 hrT2 subjects.

## Access and tier

Open via the crossMoDA challenge platform. Sign click-through agreement.

## Format and download

Native: NIfTI. Recommended: pair ceT1 + hrT2 for cycle-consistency / synthesis-based UDA approaches.

## Intended use for this corpus

Cross-modality unsupervised domain adaptation, VS surveillance imaging without gadolinium contrast, sub-segmentation models for radiation-therapy planning. Important clinical motivation: avoiding gadolinium in long-term VS follow-up.
