# OAI (Osteoarthritis Initiative) Knee MRI

The Osteoarthritis Initiative is a multi-center prospective natural-history study of knee osteoarthritis with the most comprehensive longitudinal knee MRI dataset publicly available. 4796 participants ages 45-79 were enrolled 2004-2006 with annual MRI follow-ups across 4-9 years, producing 25,729 imaging studies and approximately 26.6 million images. Four 3T scanners (one per site) acquired a harmonized protocol including sagittal 3D DESS-water-excitation (the workhorse for cartilage segmentation), sagittal T2 mapping, coronal IW-TSE, and axial PD-FS. Each visit also includes radiographs and detailed clinical/biospecimen data.

## Access and tier

Registration via NDA (NIA-managed). Application typical 1-2 weeks. No fee.

## Format and download

Native: DICOM. Recommended: DICOM-to-NIfTI conversion using `dcm2niix`. Per-visit packages downloadable via NDA download manager.

## Intended use for this corpus

Knee cartilage and meniscus segmentation, T2 mapping biomarker prediction, longitudinal OA progression (Kellgren-Lawrence grade transition), structural-clinical multimodal models. Largest open knee MRI longitudinal dataset.
