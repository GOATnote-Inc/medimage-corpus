# Calgary-Campinas-359 (Multi-Vendor Brain T1)

Calgary-Campinas-359 (CC-359) is an open multi-vendor multi-field-strength T1-weighted brain MRI dataset designed to evaluate vendor effects on quantitative analysis. It contains 359 image-domain T1 volumes from older healthy adults (29-80 years), approximately balanced across three vendors (Siemens, Philips, GE) and two field strengths (1.5T, 3T) at roughly 60 subjects per vendor x field-strength cell. Consensus brain-extraction masks are provided for all 359 volumes plus expert manual segmentations for 12 randomly selected subjects. This is the image-domain companion to the Calgary-Campinas raw k-space release.

## Access and tier

Open. Hosted on the Canada Open Neuroscience Platform (CONP).

## Format and download

Native: NIfTI. Direct download via CONP portal (https://portal.conp.ca/dataset?id=projects/calgary-campinas).

## Intended use for this corpus

Vendor-generalization brain extraction and segmentation benchmarks; cross-field-strength studies. Smaller and image-domain only - pair with the k-space release for reconstruction work.
