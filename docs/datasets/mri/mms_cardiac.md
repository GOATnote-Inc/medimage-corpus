# M&Ms Multi-Vendor Cardiac MRI Challenge

M&Ms (Multi-Centre, Multi-Vendor and Multi-Disease Cardiac Segmentation) is the canonical cross-vendor cardiac MRI benchmark. The dataset contains 350 patients (375 total exams) acquired across six clinical centers in Spain, Canada, and Germany on scanners from four vendors (Siemens, GE, Philips, Canon), covering healthy controls and patients with hypertrophic and dilated cardiomyopathies. Cine SSFP short-axis acquisitions at end-diastole and end-systole provide left ventricle (LV), myocardium (Myo), and right ventricle (RV) segmentations.

## Access and tier

Open. Hosted at https://www.ub.edu/mnms/. Click-through Data Use Agreement.

## Format and download

Native: NIfTI (cine SSFP volumes at ED and ES per patient).

## Intended use for this corpus

Cross-vendor cardiac segmentation benchmark; domain-adversarial training; cross-disease generalization. M&Ms-2 (160 additional cases focused on right-ventricular pathologies) is a separate companion release.
