# ABCD Study (Adolescent Brain Cognitive Development)

The ABCD Study is the largest long-term neuroimaging study of adolescent brain development in the United States, following 11,880 children (ages 9-10 at baseline) across 21 research sites for a planned 10 years. Each subject is scanned on a harmonized 3T protocol covering structural T1 and T2, diffusion MRI (DWI/dMRI), resting-state fMRI, and three task fMRI paradigms (MID, SST, EN-back). Baseline scans began in 2016 with biennial follow-ups, producing one of the densest longitudinal neuroimaging datasets ever assembled. The ABCD data archive is hosted by NIMH Data Archive (NDA).

## Access and tier

Application-tier. Researchers must obtain an NDA Data Use Certification (DUC) covering ABCD. Typical approval is 2-6 weeks; institutional sign-off required.

## Format and download

Native: DICOM and BIDS-compliant NIfTI. Recommended training format: BIDS+NIfTI direct from NDA download or via AWS S3 (NDA-managed bucket).

## Intended use for this corpus

Vision-encoder pretraining at the largest harmonized adolescent scale, longitudinal progression modeling (cortical thickness, white matter, functional connectivity), biomarker discovery, and substance-use/mental-health prediction. Pairs with HCP-Development for under-25 coverage.
