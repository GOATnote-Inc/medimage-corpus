# ATLAS R2.0 (Anatomical Tracings of Lesions After Stroke)

ATLAS R2.0 is the largest open T1-weighted stroke lesion segmentation MRI dataset, comprising 1271 total subjects across multiple international sites with manually-segmented lesion masks. The release splits into 655 training subjects (publicly available T1 + masks), 300 test subjects (T1 only; masks hidden), and 316 generalizability subjects (completely held out). Data are accompanied by structured metadata covering stroke etiology and chronicity.

## Access and tier

Registration. Restricted Data Use Agreement via ICPSR; complete a brief Google form to obtain the encryption code, then download from ICPSR or NITRC INDI.

## Format and download

Native: NIfTI (T1w + lesion mask pairs). Both raw native-space and preprocessed versions available.

## Intended use for this corpus

Stroke lesion segmentation, lesion-aware brain atlasing, T1-only single-modality lesion benchmarks. Complementary to ISLES (which uses DWI-based imaging for acute stroke).
