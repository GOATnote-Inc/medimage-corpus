# FeTA 2024 (Fetal Brain Tissue Annotation)

FeTA is the canonical in-utero fetal brain MRI segmentation challenge. The 2024 edition expands to approximately 200 subjects across multiple centers and is the first FeTA to include 0.55T low-field MRI data, addressing the growing relevance of point-of-care obstetric MRI. Data are super-resolution-reconstructed volumes derived from clinical T2-HASTE/SSFSE acquisitions, with 7-tissue manual annotations: external CSF, cortical gray matter, white matter, ventricles, cerebellum, brainstem, and deep gray matter. The 2024 edition introduces an automatic biometry derivation task.

## Access and tier

Registration via Grand Challenge (https://feta.grand-challenge.org/feta/). Click-through agreement.

## Format and download

Native: NIfTI super-resolution reconstructions. Recommended: NIfTI with provided 7-tissue masks.

## Intended use for this corpus

Fetal brain tissue segmentation, gestational-age regression, biometry estimation, low-field-MRI generalization. Best in-utero benchmark; pairs with dHCP for neonatal coverage.
